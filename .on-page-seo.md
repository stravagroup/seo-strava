# On-page SEO Checklist

Use this checklist before generating or publishing any blog post or service page.

Technical SEO items such as sitemaps, robots.txt, and Core Web Vitals are handled separately.

---

## 1. Head & Metadata

What Google indexes first.

- Title tag: 50–60 characters, primary keyword near the start.
- Meta description: 150–160 characters, keyword, benefit, soft CTA.
- Canonical URL set to prevent duplicates.
- Open Graph tags: og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url, og:type.
- Twitter Card tags: summary_large_image, title, description, image.
- Language attribute on HTML, for example `<html lang="en">`.
- Viewport meta tag for responsive rendering.
- Favicon included.
- Charset meta tag, for example `<meta charset="utf-8">`.

---

## 2. URL Structure

Clean, readable, keyword-forward.

- Short slug under 60 characters.
- Primary keyword in the slug.
- Hyphens only. Never underscores.
- Lowercase only.
- No stop words unless necessary.
- Logical hierarchy, for example `/services/[slug]/` or `/blog/[slug]/`.

---

## 3. Headings

Structure for skimmers and bots.

- Exactly one H1 per page containing the primary keyword.
- Logical H2 and H3 hierarchy. Never skip heading levels.
- H2s use supporting keywords and questions from the cluster.
- No keyword stuffing. Write naturally.

---

## 4. Copy & Body

Answer the query fast.

- Primary keyword in the first 100 words.
- Directly answer the query in the first paragraph.
- Length matches SERP average, ideally within 20% of the top 3.
- Short paragraphs, 1–4 sentences.
- Readability around 8th–10th grade level.
- Use active voice.
- Bold key phrases sparingly.
- Use bullets and numbered lists where appropriate.

---

## 5. FAQ Section

Every blog post should include FAQs.

- Include 4–8 questions from SEMrush Questions tab and People Also Ask.
- Direct answers, 2–4 sentences each.
- FAQ schema applied in JSON-LD.

---

## 6. Images

Every image is a ranking signal.

- Alt text describes the image and uses keywords naturally.
- Descriptive image filenames, for example `emergency-plumber-toronto.webp`.
- Use WebP and compress images under 200 KB where possible.
- Width and height attributes specified to prevent CLS.
- Lazy loading enabled using `loading="lazy"` for below-fold images.
- Responsive srcset applied where needed.
- Featured or hero image included for social sharing.

---

## 7. Internal Links

Pass authority across the site.

- Add 3–5 internal links per post.
- Link to related blog posts and relevant service pages.
- Use descriptive anchor text. Avoid “click here” or “read more”.
- Place links contextually in the body copy.
- Breadcrumbs included on every page.

---

## 8. External Links

Cite authority, do not hoard it.

- Add 2–3 external links to authoritative sources such as government, education, or major industry websites.
- Links must be relevant to the topic.
- Open external links in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`.
- Use `rel="nofollow"` for sponsored links.

---

## 9. Schema Markup

JSON-LD in the page head.

- Article schema on blog posts.
- LocalBusiness schema on service pages using the most specific business subtype where possible.
- Service schema on service pages.
- FAQ schema wherever an FAQ section exists.
- BreadcrumbList schema on every page.
- Organization schema site-wide.
- Author or Person schema for bylines.

---

## 10. E-E-A-T Signals

Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust.

- Author byline with name on every blog post.
- Author bio with credentials, years, and qualifications.
- Link to author’s dedicated page.
- Publish date displayed.
- Last updated date shown when refreshed.
- Real stories, numbers, and opinions from the business voice file.
- Cite authoritative sources.
- About page included with full company credentials.
- Contact page includes real address, phone number, and hours.

---

## 11. Accessibility

A11y signals are SEO signals.

- Use semantic HTML5: header, nav, main, article, footer.
- ARIA labels on interactive elements where needed.
- Colour contrast meets WCAG AA 4.5:1 for body text.
- Focus indicators visible on interactive elements.
- Alt text on all images. Decorative images should use empty alt text.
- Descriptive link text.
- Skip-to-content link for keyboard users.

---

## 12. Mobile & Responsive

Mobile-first indexing.

- Responsive layout.
- Touch targets minimum 48 x 48 px.
- Body font minimum 16 px.
- No horizontal scroll at any viewport.
- No intrusive interstitials.

---

## 13. Social Preview

Shareable card.

- Open Graph image optimised at 1200 x 630 and under 1 MB.
- Twitter Card image optimised at 1200 x 600.
- Compelling og:description that is different from the meta description if valuable.

---

## 14. Conversion Elements

Service pages only.

- Primary CTA above the fold.
- Phone number with click-to-call link using `tel:`.
- Multiple CTA placements throughout the page.
- Trust signals such as reviews, ratings, licences, and years in business.
- Testimonials with names and photos where possible.
- Service-area coverage listed.
- Business hours displayed.
- Physical address with embedded map where relevant.

---

## 15. Long-form Content

For posts over 1500 words.

- Table of contents with anchor links at the top.
- Jump links for each H2.
- Back-to-top button.

---

## Final Instruction

Every page, blog post, and service page must satisfy this checklist before publishing.

For service pages, include all conversion elements.

For blog posts, include FAQs, internal links, external citations, schema, author information, and strong topical coverage.

Do not publish thin, generic, or keyword-stuffed content.
